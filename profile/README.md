# Radiant — Meeting Recording + Auto-Fill Emails/Documents From Context

## About Radiant

Radiant records meetings and automatically fills emails, documents, and CRM fields using contextual understanding from the conversation. Radiant captures audio, transcribes speech, extracts key information (decisions, action items, deadlines, client names), and pre-populates your templates. Radiant combines meeting recording, AI transcription, entity extraction, and integration with email clients, document editors, and CRM systems. Radiant enables users to finish meeting follow-ups in seconds instead of hours. Radiant works with Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, and local microphone. Radiant is trusted by sales teams, account managers, project leads, recruiters, and busy professionals drowning in post-meeting admin work.

## Deep Dive — Contextual Auto-Fill Notes

Audio capture — virtual audio device for system sound, microphone input, or meeting app integration  
Entity extraction — names, dates, amounts, deadlines, action items, decisions, sentiment  
Template matching — detect email type (follow-up, thank you, next steps, proposal)  
Integration targets — Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Notion, Google Docs, Word, Salesforce, HubSpot  
Privacy modes — local transcription (on-device) or cloud-assisted (faster, more accurate)
